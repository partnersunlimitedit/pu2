About Us

Who We Are

We are a group of like-minded mid-size Logistics Providers, forming a network of Partners across the world.

What We Believer - Our Core Value

PEOPLE - PEOPLE - PEOPLE

We believe that the most important element is the PEOPLE- our members, we subscribe to

  • Regardless of the market and size of the company, all members are accepted and treated equally
  • The network is about connecting people, connecting the needs
  • The network must create value for its members
  • The best idea on how to operate the network comes from its members.

Why We Are

The objectives of the Network are:

  • To promote and develop business opportunities among the members for the mutual benefit and protect their mutual interests;
  • To enhance the members’ and Association’s market awareness and growth;
  • To represent views and interests of the members;
  • To ensure that services and facilities are provided efficiently and effectively;
  • To forge a true partnership between the members;
  • To offer to members financial security protection plan
  • To offer to our members full assistance as needed
